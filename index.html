01 — About

Quality as an engineering discipline

I treat quality as something you design in, not test at the end.

I'm a Senior QA Engineer and QA Lead based in Vienna, with 7+ years across the full testing spectrum — manual, exploratory, API, integration, system, performance, and automation. My work spans SaaS platforms, mobility products, e-government systems, and banking, always in agile, cross-functional teams.

I specialize in building automation frameworks from the ground up — repeatedly taking products from 0% to 70–80% automated coverage — while owning release readiness, risk assessment, and quality reporting for stakeholders. I've validated APIs, verified backend data flows with SQL, and run performance tests with JMeter and k6.

Beyond delivery, I've trained 350+ engineers in software testing and automation, and I'm hands-on with modern, AI-assisted QA workflows. I care about clear communication, dependable releases, and lifting the teams around me.
